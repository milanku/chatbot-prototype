# Glossary, Contact Channels & Service Levels

This document defines common banking terms used by Aurora Bank and lists mock customer contact channels and service levels.

Aurora Bank is fictional. Contact details in this file are mock details for a software exercise and do not connect to a real bank.

## 1. Emergency contact summary

Customers should use emergency channels for lost cards, suspected fraud, account takeover, or immediate security risks.

| Situation | Recommended channel | Availability |
|---|---|---|
| Lost or stolen card | Mobile app card freeze, then emergency card line | 24/7 |
| Suspected account takeover | Emergency fraud line | 24/7 |
| Unauthorized transaction | Secure message or fraud line depending on urgency | 24/7 for urgent fraud |
| Payment sent to scammer | Fraud line immediately | 24/7 |
| Card retained by ATM | Card line or secure message | 24/7 card blocking; investigation during business hours |
| Accessibility support | Inclusive Banking Team | Business hours, with appointment options |

Mock emergency card and fraud line: +421 000 123 999. This number is fictional.

## 2. Standard contact channels

| Channel | Use cases | Mock service level |
|---|---|---|
| Mobile app secure message | Account questions, document upload, disputes, non-urgent changes | Initial response within 1 business day. |
| Telephone banking | Urgent account help, identity-supported queries, card support | Typical answer target under 10 minutes. |
| Branch appointment | Identity verification, complex support, cash services, legal documents | Appointment recommended. |
| Postal mail | Formal notices, legal documents, complaints | Processing begins after receipt. |
| Chatbot | General policy guidance and transaction summaries where connected to account data | Not a substitute for legal, tax, or financial advice. |
| Video appointment | Lending discussions and accessibility support where available | Subject to booking. |

Customers should not send passwords, full card PINs, or one-time passcodes through any channel.

## 3. Definitions

### Account holder

The person legally named on the account. In a joint account, each named customer is an account holder.

### Available balance

The amount currently available for use after pending card authorizations, holds, overdraft limits, and restrictions are considered. It can differ from ledger balance.

### Beneficiary

The person or organization receiving a payment.

### Chargeback

A card-scheme process through which Aurora Bank asks the merchant’s bank to return funds for a disputed card transaction.

### Cleared funds

Funds that are available after settlement and any applicable holds or verification checks.

### Credit reference agency

A company that collects and provides credit history and identity information to lenders and other permitted organizations.

### Cutoff time

The latest time on a business day when Aurora Bank normally accepts a payment instruction for same-day processing.

### Direct debit

A payment collected by a merchant or organization from a customer’s account under a mandate authorized by the customer.

### Dynamic currency conversion

A merchant or ATM service that offers to convert a foreign-currency transaction into the customer’s home currency at a rate set by the merchant or ATM operator.

### IBAN

International Bank Account Number, used to identify bank accounts for domestic and cross-border payments in many countries.

### Ledger balance

The posted account balance after transactions already booked to the account. It may not reflect pending authorizations.

### Merchant

A business or organization accepting payment for goods, services, subscriptions, donations, or other charges.

### Overdraft

A credit facility that allows a current account balance to go below zero, subject to approval and terms.

### Pending transaction

A transaction that has been authorized or requested but not fully posted or settled.

### Pre-authorization

A temporary hold placed by a merchant before the final transaction amount is known, common for hotels, car rentals, and fuel stations.

### Recall

An attempt to recover a bank transfer after it has been sent. A recall is not guaranteed.

### Strong customer authentication

Authentication using at least two factors, such as password, device, and biometric verification.

### Temporary credit

A provisional refund applied during a dispute investigation. It can be reversed if the claim is unsuccessful.

### Unarranged overdraft

A negative balance that occurs without an approved overdraft or above the arranged overdraft limit.

## 4. Service levels for common requests

| Request | Typical service level | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Debit card replacement | 5-7 business days by standard post | Emergency courier may be available for a fee. |
| PIN reminder or reset | Immediate in app where available; otherwise 3-5 business days | Security checks required. |
| Standard SEPA payment investigation | Initial review within 3 business days | Receiving bank timelines vary. |
| International payment trace | 5-15 business days | Correspondent banks may take longer. |
| Card chargeback | 30-90 calendar days typical | Can take longer if merchant challenges. |
| Direct debit cancellation | Same day if before cutoff | Customer should also contact merchant. |
| Loan application decision | Instant to 5 business days | Complex/self-employed cases may take longer. |
| Data subject access request | Within 1 month unless extended by law | Identity verification required. |
| Complaint final response | See complaint policy | Depends on complaint type and law. |
| Account closure | 1-5 business days if no restrictions | Pending transactions or debts can delay. |

## 5. Secure communication rules

Aurora Bank may use SMS, email, app push notification, secure message, phone, post, or branch communication. Not every message asking a customer to act is genuine. Customers should access online banking by typing the known address or opening the official app, not by following unexpected links.

Aurora Bank may send one-time passcodes but will never ask the customer to disclose a passcode to approve an action the customer did not initiate.

## 6. Language and translation

Aurora Bank’s official customer documents are provided in the account language selected by the customer where available. Translations may be provided for convenience. If there is a conflict, the legally binding language version in the product agreement prevails.

## 7. Legal, tax, and financial advice

Aurora Bank can explain its products and processes, but it does not provide independent legal, tax, immigration, investment, or debt advice unless a separate regulated advisory service is agreed. Customers should seek independent advice for personal legal, tax, or financial planning decisions.
