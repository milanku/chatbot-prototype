# Current & Savings Accounts Terms

This document explains the main operating rules for Aurora Bank personal current accounts and savings accounts. Account opening requirements are described in [Account Opening, Eligibility & Customer Verification](01_account_opening_and_kyc.md). Fees are described in [Fees, Interest & Foreign Exchange](06_fees_interest_and_exchange_rates.md).

## 1. Available personal account types

Aurora Bank offers the following mock personal account types.

| Account | Intended use | Main features | Credit facility |
|---|---|---|---|
| Aurora Everyday Current Account | Salary, bills, card spending, regular transfers | Debit card, online banking, SEPA transfers, direct debits, standing orders, optional overdraft | Optional arranged overdraft, subject to approval |
| Aurora Basic Payment Account | Essential day-to-day payments | Debit card, salary receipt, SEPA transfers, cash access, no monthly package fee | No overdraft |
| Aurora Plus Account | Day-to-day banking with added services | Travel card controls, priority support, extra ATM allowance, document replacement support | Optional arranged overdraft, subject to approval |
| Aurora Youth Current Account | Customers aged 15 to 17 | Debit card with lower limits, guardian notifications, no credit | No overdraft |
| Aurora Instant Saver | Flexible savings | Variable interest, transfers to nominated Aurora account | No credit |
| Aurora Notice Saver | Savings with notice period | Higher variable interest than Instant Saver, withdrawals require notice | No credit |
| Aurora Fixed Term Deposit | Lump sum savings | Fixed rate for agreed term, early closure restrictions | No credit |

Product availability can change. A customer’s product terms prevail over this general guide if there is a conflict.

## 2. Current account core services

A current account normally allows the customer to:

- receive salary, pension, benefits, refunds, and other incoming payments;
- make SEPA credit transfers and, where available, instant payments;
- set up standing orders and direct debits;
- use a debit card for purchases, cash withdrawals, and online payments;
- use mobile wallet services where the device and card are eligible;
- view balances, statements, and payment confirmations;
- send secure messages to Aurora Bank;
- request account documents such as balance confirmations or interest certificates.

A current account is not a safe-deposit service, investment product, escrow arrangement, or business merchant-processing account.

## 3. Savings account core services

Savings accounts are designed for storing money and earning interest. They are not designed for high-frequency payments. A savings account may restrict debit cards, direct debits, standing orders, third-party incoming payments, international payments, or cash withdrawals.

### 3.1 Instant Saver

The Instant Saver allows transfers to and from a nominated Aurora current account. Interest is variable and may change after notice. Withdrawals can normally be made at any time, but large withdrawals may require additional security checks.

### 3.2 Notice Saver

The Notice Saver requires notice before withdrawal. A 32-day notice account requires 32 calendar days’ notice; a 90-day notice account requires 90 calendar days’ notice. Aurora Bank may permit early withdrawal only in exceptional circumstances, such as serious illness, death, or legal obligation. An early-withdrawal interest adjustment may apply.

### 3.3 Fixed Term Deposit

A Fixed Term Deposit is opened for a fixed period and pays a fixed interest rate. Additional deposits and partial withdrawals are not normally allowed after the funding window closes. Early closure is not normally available unless required by law or approved due to hardship.

## 4. Account balances

The **ledger balance** is the balance after transactions already posted to the account. The **available balance** is the amount available for use after pending transactions, card authorizations, overdraft limits, account restrictions, and holds are considered.

A card purchase may reduce the available balance immediately but post to the ledger balance later. A hotel, car rental, fuel station, or online merchant may place a pre-authorization that is higher than the final amount. Pre-authorizations are explained in [Cards, Debit Cards, Credit Cards, ATMs & Mobile Wallets](04_cards_debit_credit_atm_mobile_wallets.md).

## 5. Statements and notices

Aurora Bank provides monthly statements for current accounts and at least annual statements for savings accounts unless the product terms provide more frequent statements. Customers can access statements in online banking for at least seven years after issue, unless the account was closed and retention periods have expired.

Customers may request paper statements. A paper statement fee may apply unless the customer needs paper statements as an accessibility adjustment or local law requires free paper copies.

Important account notices may be delivered through online banking, secure message, email, SMS notification, postal mail, or branch notice. Customers must keep contact details up to date.

## 6. Overdraft basics

An arranged overdraft is a credit facility attached to a current account. It allows the balance to go below zero up to an approved limit. Arranged overdrafts are subject to credit assessment and are not guaranteed. Details are in [Loans, Overdrafts & Credit Assessment](05_loans_overdrafts_and_credit_assessment.md).

An unarranged overdraft occurs when the account goes below zero without approval or exceeds an arranged limit. Aurora Bank may refuse payments that would create or increase an unarranged overdraft. Fees and interest may apply as described in [Fees, Interest & Foreign Exchange](06_fees_interest_and_exchange_rates.md).

Youth accounts, Basic Payment Accounts, savings accounts, and Fixed Term Deposits do not offer arranged overdrafts.

## 7. Account packages and fee waivers

Packaged accounts may include non-banking benefits, enhanced service channels, higher ATM fee allowances, or travel-related features. Benefits may be provided by third parties and may have separate eligibility terms.

Aurora Bank may waive or reduce account fees for students, salary-account customers, seniors, customers receiving qualifying benefits, or customers who maintain a minimum monthly balance. Fee waivers are not automatic unless stated in the product terms.

## 8. Direct debits, standing orders, and recurring payments

Direct debits are authorized by the customer to allow a payee to collect variable or fixed amounts. Standing orders are instructions created by the customer to pay a fixed amount on a schedule. Recurring card payments are card-authorized merchant arrangements and are not the same as direct debits.

Cancellation rules differ:

- A standing order can normally be cancelled in online banking before the processing cutoff.
- A direct debit can be cancelled with Aurora Bank, but the customer should also cancel the agreement with the merchant.
- A recurring card payment should be cancelled with the merchant. Aurora Bank can block future merchant attempts in some cases, but this may not cancel the underlying contract.

Payment execution and cancellation timings are described in [Payments, Transfers & Cutoff Times](03_payments_transfers_and_cutoff_times.md).

## 9. Cash deposits and withdrawals

Cash services may be available at Aurora branches, partner branches, selected ATMs, and approved agent locations. Limits may apply based on product type, customer profile, channel, and security risk.

Aurora Bank may ask for information about large or unusual cash deposits. Cash deposits can be rejected where required by law, where notes appear counterfeit, where the source of funds cannot be reasonably explained, or where cash activity is inconsistent with personal account use.

## 10. Dormant and inactive accounts

An account may be treated as inactive if there has been no customer-initiated transaction or customer contact for 12 months. It may be treated as dormant after a longer period defined by local law or product terms.

Before restricting a dormant account, Aurora Bank will make reasonable efforts to contact the customer using the contact details on record. Dormant-account processes, reactivation, and estates handling are explained in [Account Closure, Inactivity, Estates & Power of Attorney](11_account_closure_inactivity_estates_and_power_of_attorney.md).

## 11. Account switching

Customers may request to switch payment arrangements from another bank to Aurora Bank. Depending on the market and scheme availability, switching may include transfer of incoming salary, standing orders, direct debits, and closing balance. Aurora Bank cannot guarantee that all merchants or employers will update their records immediately.

Customers should keep enough funds in both old and new accounts during the transition period. Missed payment charges from other providers are not automatically refunded by Aurora Bank.

## 12. Account restrictions

Aurora Bank may restrict an account where:

- security is at risk;
- suspicious or unauthorized activity is detected;
- the customer has not provided required information;
- a court order, tax order, enforcement order, sanctions requirement, or legal notice applies;
- the account is being used outside product terms;
- a dispute between joint account holders cannot be resolved;
- the customer is insolvent or subject to a legal process.

We will inform the customer where we are legally allowed to do so. Restrictions can affect cards, online banking, payments, cash withdrawals, statements, and account closure.

## 13. Changes to terms

Aurora Bank may change account terms, fees, interest rates, payment limits, security requirements, or service channels. We will provide notice where required. A customer may close or switch the account before a disadvantageous change takes effect, unless the change is required immediately for legal, regulatory, or security reasons.
