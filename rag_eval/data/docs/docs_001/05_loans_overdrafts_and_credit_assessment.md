# Loans, Overdrafts & Credit Assessment

This document explains how Aurora Bank handles personal loan requests, overdrafts, credit-card applications, and credit assessments. It does not replace a regulated credit agreement. Fees and interest are described in [Fees, Interest & Foreign Exchange](06_fees_interest_and_exchange_rates.md).

## 1. Lending products covered

Aurora Bank may offer the following consumer credit products:

| Product | Typical purpose | Repayment style | Security |
|---|---|---|---|
| Personal loan | Car, home improvement, education, debt consolidation, major purchase | Fixed monthly instalments | Usually unsecured |
| Arranged overdraft | Short-term cash-flow buffer linked to current account | Repayable on demand; interest charged on used balance | Unsecured |
| Credit card | Flexible purchases and short-term borrowing | Minimum monthly payment; revolving balance | Unsecured |
| Mortgage referral or home loan | Property purchase or refinancing | Monthly instalments over long term | Secured on property |
| Green home improvement loan | Energy-efficiency upgrades | Fixed instalments | Usually unsecured, invoices may be required |

Product availability depends on jurisdiction, customer profile, and regulatory permission.

## 2. General eligibility

To request credit, a customer normally must:

- be at least 18 years old;
- hold or be eligible for an Aurora Bank current account;
- have verified identity and address;
- provide income and employment information;
- pass credit, affordability, fraud, and regulatory checks;
- not be bankrupt, subject to certain insolvency proceedings, or in unresolved serious arrears;
- use the credit for a lawful personal purpose.

Aurora Bank may apply stricter rules for high-value loans, secured lending, non-resident customers, self-employed customers, or customers with irregular income.

## 3. How to request a loan

A customer can request a loan through online banking, the mobile app, telephone banking, or a branch appointment. The request normally includes:

1. requested amount;
2. requested term;
3. purpose of borrowing;
4. employment status;
5. income and regular expenses;
6. housing costs;
7. existing debts and credit commitments;
8. dependants and household circumstances;
9. consent to credit checks;
10. supporting documents where required.

An indicative quote is not a final approval. Final approval is subject to verification, affordability assessment, credit checks, and signing the credit agreement.

## 4. Documents for employed customers

Employed customers may be asked for:

- recent payslips;
- employment contract or employer confirmation;
- bank statements showing salary credits;
- tax certificate or annual income statement;
- proof of address;
- details of other credit commitments.

Where salary is paid into an Aurora current account, fewer documents may be required, but Aurora Bank may still request evidence.

## 5. Documents for self-employed customers

Self-employed customers may be asked for:

- recent tax returns;
- business bank statements;
- personal bank statements;
- invoices or contracts;
- accountant confirmation;
- trade registration or company register extract;
- profit and loss statement;
- evidence of recurring customer income.

Self-employed income may be averaged over a period. Seasonal or irregular income may require additional explanation. Aurora Bank may decline a loan if income cannot be verified or appears unstable.

## 6. Credit assessment factors

Aurora Bank assesses applications using a combination of automated and manual review. Factors may include:

- verified income and disposable income;
- stability and source of income;
- existing debts and repayment history;
- current account conduct;
- credit bureau records;
- recent credit applications;
- arrears, defaults, insolvencies, or court judgments;
- requested amount and term;
- loan purpose;
- customer age and expected retirement age for longer terms;
- fraud and identity risk indicators;
- regulatory affordability requirements.

Approval is not guaranteed even where a customer has a good credit score. Aurora Bank must also consider affordability and responsible lending.

## 7. Loan purpose and restrictions

Aurora Bank may ask for the purpose of borrowing. We do not normally provide personal loans for:

- unlawful activity;
- speculative investment, including unregulated crypto-asset speculation;
- gambling;
- business working capital through a personal loan;
- payment of another person’s debt where the customer receives no benefit;
- weapons, sanctions-restricted goods, or prohibited trade;
- tax avoidance or evasion schemes.

Debt consolidation may be allowed, but Aurora Bank may pay creditors directly or require evidence that old debts were settled.

## 8. Interest rates and representative examples

Loan interest rates can be fixed or variable depending on product terms. The final rate may depend on credit assessment, loan amount, term, product type, collateral, account relationship, and market conditions.

A representative example is only an illustration. It does not guarantee that a customer will receive that rate. Interest, annual percentage rate, arrangement fees, early-repayment charges, and late-payment charges are described in the customer’s credit agreement and [Fees, Interest & Foreign Exchange](06_fees_interest_and_exchange_rates.md).

## 9. Arranged overdrafts

An arranged overdraft is intended as a short-term borrowing facility. It should not be used for long-term borrowing or regular living costs. Aurora Bank may review, reduce, suspend, or cancel an overdraft after notice or immediately where required for risk, fraud, legal, or account-conduct reasons.

Important overdraft rules:

- Interest is charged only on the used arranged overdraft balance.
- The overdraft is repayable on demand.
- Exceeding the limit may lead to declined payments and fees.
- An overdraft limit is not a promise that every payment will be honored.
- Savings accounts and youth accounts do not have overdrafts.

## 10. Credit cards

A credit-card application is assessed separately from a current account. Aurora Bank sets a credit limit and may change it after review. Customers can request a lower limit at any time. Limit increases require affordability and conduct checks.

Credit-card statements show the statement balance, minimum payment, payment due date, interest, fees, and transactions. Paying only the minimum payment increases the cost and time needed to repay. Cash advances and certain transactions may attract immediate interest and fees.

Card operational rules are in [Cards, Debit Cards, Credit Cards, ATMs & Mobile Wallets](04_cards_debit_credit_atm_mobile_wallets.md).

## 11. Repayment

Loan repayments are normally collected monthly by direct debit from an Aurora current account or another accepted account. The customer must ensure there is enough available balance before the due date.

If a payment is missed, Aurora Bank may:

- contact the customer;
- charge late-payment fees where permitted;
- report arrears to credit reference agencies;
- reduce or cancel further borrowing;
- collect from available linked accounts only where the agreement permits set-off;
- start collections or legal action as a last resort.

Customers who expect difficulty should contact Aurora Bank before missing a payment.

## 12. Early repayment and overpayments

Customers may be able to repay a loan early in full or make overpayments. Early repayment can reduce total interest but may trigger an early-repayment charge if allowed by law and the credit agreement.

For partial overpayments, Aurora Bank may reduce the remaining term, reduce monthly payments, or hold the overpayment until recalculation, depending on product terms and customer instruction.

## 13. Payment holidays and hardship support

Aurora Bank may offer temporary support if a customer is experiencing financial difficulty. Support can include payment deferral, temporary reduced payments, interest-only periods, debt advice referral, breathing-space arrangements, or restructuring.

Support is not automatic and may affect credit records, total interest, product eligibility, or future borrowing. Customers should provide accurate information about income, expenses, assets, debts, and the cause of difficulty.

Vulnerable-customer support is described in [Complaints, Vulnerable Customers & Accessibility](10_complaints_vulnerable_customers_and_accessibility.md).

## 14. Loan decline reasons

A loan request may be declined because:

- income is insufficient or cannot be verified;
- the requested repayments are not affordable;
- credit history shows missed payments, defaults, or insolvency;
- the customer has too many recent credit applications;
- account conduct indicates risk;
- documents are missing or inconsistent;
- fraud or identity concerns exist;
- the loan purpose is restricted;
- the customer does not meet product criteria;
- legal or regulatory rules prevent lending.

Aurora Bank will provide a general reason where possible. We may not disclose detailed fraud, model, or internal-risk information.

## 15. Refinancing and debt consolidation

Refinancing means replacing existing debt with a new loan. Debt consolidation can simplify repayments but may increase total cost if the term is longer or fees apply. Customers should compare total repayable amount, interest rate, fees, and secured/unsecured status.

Aurora Bank may require evidence that existing debts will be repaid. For some consolidation loans, funds may be sent directly to other lenders.

## 16. Credit data and privacy

Aurora Bank may share repayment data with credit reference agencies and fraud prevention databases. This can affect future credit applications. Data rights and automated decision information are described in [Privacy, Data Protection & Open Banking](08_privacy_data_protection_and_open_banking.md).
