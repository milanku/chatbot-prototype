You are judging questions for a customer-facing banking RAG evaluation dataset.

For each question, decide whether it should be included.

### ACCEPT

Accept questions that represent a realistic information need a real customer could reasonably have when interacting with a banking assistant.

Questions can be:

* specific or broad
* simple or complex
* procedural, informational, or scenario-based
* about policies, products, services, fees, rates, eligibility, processes, or what to do in a situation
* asking for specific information contained in the knowledge base
* asking where the customer can find information they need
* referring to a document, section, or source by name when this helps the customer obtain the information they need
* combining multiple closely related information needs
* imperfectly worded or containing mistakes

Do not require the question to use a particular phrasing or question type.

A question should be judged by the customer's underlying information need, not by whether it mentions a document or where information can be found.

For example:

* "What should I do if my card is stolen?" → ACCEPT
* "Can I get help if I'm having difficulty managing my finances?" → ACCEPT
* "How does interest work differently for loans and savings?" → ACCEPT
* "In which document can I find information about fees?" → ACCEPT
* "If my card is lost or stolen and I see an unauthorized transaction, what should I do right away, and where are the emergency contact options listed in the Glossary, Contact Channels & Service Levels?" → ACCEPT

### REJECT

Reject questions that refer primarily about the documents themselves, rather than information the customer needs or they contain.

Reject questions about document metadata or administrative information, including:

* document IDs
* document titles when the title itself is the requested information
* filenames
* document versions
* effective or publication dates
* document owners or internal departments
* document classification
* what a document contains or covers when there is no specific customer information need
* internal document structure when it is not useful for obtaining information the customer needs

For example:

* "Who owns this policy?" → REJECT
* "What does the effective date 2026-01-01 refer to in this customer guidance?" → REJECT
* "What is the title of the document with id AB-CUST-PAY-2026-01?" → REJECT
* "What is the document ID for the payments policy?" → REJECT

Focus on the **customer's information need**, not on whether the question is perfectly written.

Return exactly one result for every question.

Use the original question ID as `question_id`.

Set `quality` to `ACCEPT` or `REJECT`.

For `reason`, briefly explain why the question is suitable or unsuitable.

For each question, provide:

* `question_id`
* `quality`: `ACCEPT`, `REJECT`
* `reason`: a concise explanation why the question was accepted or rejected

Return the results using the following structured output format:

{
  "results": [
    "question_id": str,
    "quality": "ACCEPT" | "REJECT"
    "reason": str
  ]
}
