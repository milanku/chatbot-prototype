# Complaints, Vulnerable Customers & Accessibility

This document explains how Aurora Bank handles complaints and supports customers who need additional help. Contact routes and service levels are listed in [Glossary, Contact Channels & Service Levels](12_glossary_contact_and_service_level.md).

## 1. What counts as a complaint

A complaint is an expression of dissatisfaction about Aurora Bank’s products, services, staff, systems, fees, decisions, delays, or complaint handling. A customer does not need to use the word "complaint" for Aurora Bank to treat it as one.

Examples include:

- a payment delay caused by bank error;
- a fee the customer believes is wrong;
- poor branch or telephone service;
- declined card or payment where the customer believes the decision was wrong;
- disagreement with a fraud or chargeback outcome;
- accessibility needs not met;
- concerns about data privacy;
- concerns about lending affordability or collections behavior.

## 2. How to make a complaint

Customers can complain through:

- secure message in online banking;
- telephone banking;
- branch appointment;
- postal letter;
- email to the complaints team where available;
- an authorized representative, attorney, guardian, or support worker;
- accessibility-assisted channel.

The customer should provide account details, contact details, what happened, relevant dates, amounts, names, evidence, and the outcome requested. Customers should not send passwords, PINs, or one-time passcodes.

## 3. Complaint acknowledgement and response times

Aurora Bank will acknowledge complaints promptly. Where possible, we resolve simple complaints within three business days. More complex complaints may take longer.

Indicative response standards:

| Complaint type | Expected handling |
|---|---|
| Simple service issue | Aim to resolve within 3 business days. |
| Standard complaint | Written final response normally within 15 business days for payment-service complaints, or within 30 calendar days for other complaints where local rules allow. |
| Complex complaint | If more time is needed, Aurora Bank will explain the delay and provide a final response within the maximum period required by law. |
| Fraud, chargeback, or scam complaint | Investigation timelines depend on scheme rules, evidence, receiving banks, and law enforcement. |
| Privacy complaint | Handled with input from the Data Protection Office. |

The final response will explain the outcome, any redress, and escalation rights where applicable.

## 4. What Aurora Bank may do to resolve a complaint

Depending on the facts, Aurora Bank may:

- apologize;
- correct an error;
- refund a fee or interest;
- make a goodwill payment;
- update records;
- reopen an investigation;
- provide staff feedback or training;
- improve a process;
- explain why the original decision was correct;
- refer the customer to an external dispute resolution body.

Aurora Bank does not provide compensation for every inconvenience, and redress depends on evidence, legal obligations, financial loss, distress, and fairness.

## 5. Escalation and external review

If the customer is not satisfied with the final response, they may be able to refer the complaint to an ombudsman, financial services mediator, data protection authority, court, or regulator depending on complaint type and jurisdiction. The final response will include relevant escalation information where required.

Customers usually must complain to Aurora Bank before using an external dispute resolution body.

## 6. Vulnerable customer support

A customer may be vulnerable because of permanent, temporary, or situational circumstances. Examples include disability, serious illness, bereavement, mental health difficulty, addiction, financial abuse, domestic abuse, low literacy, language barriers, digital exclusion, unemployment, displacement, or sudden income shock.

Aurora Bank can offer support such as:

- alternative communication formats;
- extra time to provide documents;
- branch appointments in private rooms;
- third-party support with customer permission;
- trusted contact notes;
- temporary card or payment controls;
- debt-support referral;
- priority handling for urgent hardship;
- interpreter or relay services;
- simplified explanations of fees, credit, and repayment options.

Support is tailored to circumstances and product rules. Some requests may require evidence or legal authority.

## 7. Accessibility adjustments

Aurora Bank can provide reasonable adjustments such as:

- large print, Braille, audio, or accessible PDF documents;
- telephone relay support;
- sign-language appointment where available;
- step-free branch appointment where possible;
- longer appointment slots;
- secure message instead of phone call;
- assistance reading forms;
- accessible card design or tactile card features where offered;
- paper statements without fee where digital access is not suitable because of disability.

Customers can request adjustments at any time. Some formats may take longer to prepare.

## 8. Third-party authority and support persons

A customer can ask Aurora Bank to communicate with a support person. The level of access depends on authority provided:

| Authority type | What it usually allows |
|---|---|
| Trusted contact | Aurora Bank may contact the person if concerned about customer welfare or financial harm; no transaction authority. |
| One-time verbal authority | Discussion of a specific matter during a call or appointment. |
| Third-party mandate | Ongoing account discussion or limited account operation, depending on mandate. |
| Power of attorney | Legal authority according to the document and local law. |
| Guardian or court-appointed representative | Authority defined by court or law. |

Power of attorney and estates processes are described in [Account Closure, Inactivity, Estates & Power of Attorney](11_account_closure_inactivity_estates_and_power_of_attorney.md).

## 9. Financial difficulty

Customers who cannot pay bills, loan repayments, overdrafts, or credit-card balances should contact Aurora Bank as early as possible. Early contact gives more options.

Support may include:

- income and expenditure review;
- temporary payment arrangement;
- overdraft review;
- breathing-space or moratorium process where available;
- referral to free debt advice;
- freezing certain charges where appropriate;
- restructuring or term extension where allowed;
- collections hold while evidence is reviewed.

Financial difficulty support may affect credit reporting depending on the arrangement.

## 10. Bereavement support

When a customer dies, Aurora Bank will explain next steps to the representative or family. We may need a death certificate, proof of identity for the representative, grant of probate or equivalent document, and instructions for account closure or transfer.

Further details are in [Account Closure, Inactivity, Estates & Power of Attorney](11_account_closure_inactivity_estates_and_power_of_attorney.md).

## 11. Domestic abuse and financial abuse

Aurora Bank recognizes that financial abuse can include controlling access to money, coercing loans, intercepting cards, monitoring statements, forcing transfers, or misusing joint accounts.

Customers can request confidential support. Aurora Bank can discuss safe contact methods, correspondence suppression, card replacement, account separation options, payment controls, and referral to specialist organizations. Joint account changes may require legal or operational safeguards.

## 12. Staff conduct

Customers and staff are expected to treat each other respectfully. Aurora Bank may restrict communication channels if a customer uses abusive, threatening, discriminatory, or persistently unreasonable behavior. We will still try to preserve access to essential banking services where safe and lawful.
