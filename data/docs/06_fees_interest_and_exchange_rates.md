# Fees, Interest & Foreign Exchange

This document summarizes common Aurora Bank fees, interest practices, and foreign exchange rules for personal customers. Product-specific agreements may contain additional fees or preferential pricing. The amounts below are mock values for a fictional bank.

## 1. General fee principles

Aurora Bank will apply fees only where they are shown in the applicable fee schedule, product terms, or customer agreement. We may waive a fee where required by law, because of bank error, as a goodwill gesture, or for accessibility and vulnerability reasons.

Customers are responsible for maintaining enough available balance to pay fees, interest, repayments, and scheduled payments. A fee can create or increase an overdraft where product terms allow this.

## 5. Overdraft fees and interest

Arranged overdraft interest is calculated daily on the used arranged overdraft balance and charged monthly. The illustrative arranged overdraft rate is 13.90% annual nominal variable.

Aurora Bank does not charge a separate daily unarranged overdraft fee in this mock fee schedule. However, payments that exceed the available balance may be rejected and failed-payment fees may apply where permitted. If an unarranged negative balance occurs, debit interest may be charged and the customer must repay it immediately.

The bank may reduce or waive fees where financial difficulty is confirmed. Hardship support is described in [Loans, Overdrafts & Credit Assessment](05_loans_overdrafts_and_credit_assessment.md).

## 7. Savings interest

Savings interest can be fixed or variable. Variable rates may change after notice. Fixed Term Deposit rates are fixed for the term once the deposit is opened and funded.

Illustrative annual gross rates:

| Product | Interest rate type | Indicative annual gross rate |
|---|---|---:|
| Instant Saver | Variable | 1.20% |
| 32-Day Notice Saver | Variable | 1.85% |
| 90-Day Notice Saver | Variable | 2.10% |
| 12-Month Fixed Term Deposit | Fixed | 2.40% |

Interest is normally calculated daily on cleared balance and paid monthly or annually depending on product terms. Tax may be withheld or reported depending on the customer’s tax status and local law.

## 8. Current account interest

Aurora Everyday Current Account and Basic Payment Account do not normally pay credit interest. Promotional or packaged accounts may pay interest on balances up to a stated cap. Interest eligibility can depend on minimum monthly deposits, direct debit activity, product package, or residency.

## 9. Foreign exchange rates

Foreign exchange may apply to card transactions, international transfers, cash withdrawals, refunds, and account conversions.

Aurora Bank may use:

- a card scheme wholesale rate plus Aurora Bank FX spread;
- an Aurora Bank reference exchange rate quoted before payment;
- a treasury rate for larger transfers;
- a rate provided by a third-party payment partner;
- a merchant or ATM dynamic currency conversion rate where the customer chooses it.

Rates can change continuously. The rate shown before authorization may differ from the final settlement rate for card transactions.

## 10. Dynamic currency conversion

Dynamic currency conversion occurs when a merchant or ATM offers to charge the customer in the customer’s home currency instead of the local currency. The displayed home-currency amount may include a markup set by the merchant or ATM operator.

Aurora Bank generally recommends that customers compare rates before choosing. Selecting the local currency usually means Aurora Bank or the card scheme performs conversion. Selecting the home currency usually means the merchant or ATM operator performs conversion.

## 11. Refunds in foreign currency

A foreign-currency refund may not exactly equal the original home-currency charge because exchange rates can move between purchase and refund dates. Merchant fees, partial refunds, and chargeback outcomes can also affect the amount received.

## 13. Fee disputes

Customers who believe a fee was charged incorrectly should contact Aurora Bank with the transaction date, amount, account, and reason for dispute. Fees caused by bank error will be refunded. Fees caused by merchant disputes, failed payments, or insufficient funds are assessed according to the relevant product terms.

The complaints process is described in [Complaints, Vulnerable Customers & Accessibility](10_complaints_vulnerable_customers_and_accessibility.md).

## 14. Changes to fees and rates

Aurora Bank may change fees and variable rates after notice. Some changes can take effect immediately where required by law, security, payment-scheme rules, tax, or regulatory change. Customers may close or switch an account before a disadvantageous change takes effect unless a fixed-term or credit agreement restricts closure.
